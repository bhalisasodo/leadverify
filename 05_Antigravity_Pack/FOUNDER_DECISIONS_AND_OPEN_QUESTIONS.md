# LeadVerify — Founder Decisions & Open Questions

Do not silently resolve these questions in code. Use provisional configuration where possible and ask founders before hard-coding.

## Brand and identity
- Approved logo, colour palette, typography, and brand voice?
- Public domain and deployment target?
- Is there an existing repository or prototype to preserve?

## Geography and currency
- Is the first pilot South Africa only?
- Is ZAR the sole initial currency?
- Which countries/time zones must the MVP support?

## Business verification
- Which company verification checks are required?
- Who reviews and approves businesses?
- What evidence is mandatory?

## Agent verification
- Which identity verification method/provider?
- Are reference checks mandatory?
- Which assessments are required per role?
- Minimum age and eligibility policy?
- Who can see verification results and documents?
- How long is verification valid?

## Campaign commercial policy
- Minimum activation fee, if any?
- Minimum contribution margin or campaign approval threshold?
- Who can approve an exception?
- Standard payment terms?
- When does agent compensation become payable?
- Is agent payment conditional on client collection for any commission campaign, and is that legally permissible?
- Reserve/holdback policy for refunds and reversals?
- Standard attribution window and dispute process?

## Operations
- Who owns client success?
- Who approves campaigns?
- Who reviews deliverables?
- Who approves payouts?
- What service-level targets will be offered?
- Which CRM and communication tools are already in use?

## Technology
- Confirm Supabase or alternative backend.
- Confirm hosting provider.
- Confirm email provider.
- Confirm identity verification provider.
- Confirm analytics/error monitoring.
- Confirm whether payment integration is required in MVP or only manual reconciliation.

## Legal and compliance
- Obtain review of POPIA responsibilities and direct marketing.
- Obtain review of contractor classification.
- Obtain review of client and agent agreements.
- Identify regulated sales categories and prohibited campaigns.
- Confirm data retention and deletion schedule.
- Confirm complaint and dispute handling.

## Product policy
- Can agents apply to all opportunities or only be assigned by admins?
- Can agents see client identity before assignment?
- What client information can agents see?
- Can clients reject a deliverable, and what is the appeal process?
- How are duplicate leads handled?
- What is the definition of an attended appointment?
- Which sales outcomes count toward commission?
