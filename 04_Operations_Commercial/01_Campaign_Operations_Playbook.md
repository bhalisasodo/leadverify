# LeadVerify — Campaign Operations Playbook

## 1. Purpose

Standardise how LeadVerify evaluates, approves, staffs, runs, reviews, and closes managed sales campaigns.

## 2. Campaign intake checklist

### Client and product
- Business identity and contact verified.
- Website and product/service reviewed.
- Product claims and pricing understood.
- Fulfilment capacity confirmed.
- Existing customer evidence or traction discussed.
- Regulated activity identified.

### Sales proposition
- Ideal customer profile documented.
- Target geography defined.
- Decision-maker roles defined.
- Value proposition and differentiators documented.
- Common objections understood.
- Existing sales process reviewed.
- Sales materials and scripts obtained.

### Scope
- Services selected.
- Deliverables defined.
- Acceptance criteria documented.
- Volume and quality targets set.
- Channels approved.
- Campaign duration and milestones set.
- Client responsibilities documented.

### Commercial viability
- Pricing/commission verified.
- Agent compensation model defined.
- Direct campaign costs estimated.
- Management and QA costs estimated.
- Payment timing and collection risk assessed.
- Attribution and reversal rules agreed.
- Contribution margin assessed.
- Working-capital exposure approved.

### Data and compliance
- Data sources documented.
- Access permissions and data-sharing terms agreed.
- Privacy notice / lawful basis responsibilities clarified.
- Outreach channel rules reviewed.
- Opt-out and suppression process defined.
- Regulated product checks completed.

## 3. Campaign staffing

1. Translate campaign requirements into required roles and skills.
2. Filter approved agents by skill, experience, language, geography, availability, and capacity.
3. Review assessment and performance evidence.
4. Confirm agent interest and compensation acceptance.
5. Assign agents and define responsibilities.
6. Provide campaign briefing and training.
7. Confirm access permissions and tools.
8. Record assignment acceptance.

## 4. Campaign briefing template

- Client and product summary.
- Campaign objective.
- Ideal customer profile.
- Geography and exclusions.
- Required agent role.
- Deliverables and acceptance criteria.
- Approved scripts and messaging.
- Approved channels.
- Client-specific claims agents may and may not make.
- Escalation path.
- Data handling and security rules.
- Compensation terms and payout conditions.
- Reporting frequency.
- Campaign dates and milestones.

## 5. Quality assurance

- Validate lead fields.
- Check duplicates using agreed campaign rules.
- Verify relevance to target customer profile.
- Review qualification evidence.
- Check outreach history and channel compliance.
- Validate appointment qualification and attendance where applicable.
- Record acceptance/rejection reason.
- Provide corrective feedback.
- Monitor repeat quality issues by agent and campaign.

## 6. Campaign reporting

Minimum client report:
- Reporting period.
- Campaign objective and scope.
- Work completed.
- Leads submitted.
- Leads accepted/rejected and reasons.
- Qualified opportunities.
- Appointments booked/confirmed/attended.
- Sales outcomes, where available and attributable.
- KPI performance.
- Risks and blockers.
- Planned next actions.

## 7. Campaign closeout

- Confirm final deliverables.
- Resolve outstanding quality reviews.
- Reconcile client invoices and receipts.
- Reconcile agent compensation.
- Record disputes, reversals, and holds.
- Revoke unnecessary access.
- Archive or delete data according to retention policy.
- Capture client feedback.
- Capture agent feedback.
- Record lessons and campaign benchmarks.
- Discuss renewal or expansion.
