# LeadVerify — Commercial & Compensation Framework

## 1. Commercial principles

- LeadVerify contracts with and manages the client.
- Agent compensation is agreed before an assignment begins.
- Campaign economics are assessed before acceptance.
- Deliverables and acceptance criteria are explicit.
- Client payment obligations and agent payment conditions are transparent.
- No future commission is treated as collected cash.
- Every financial event is auditable.
- No universal agent/LeadVerify revenue split is assumed.

## 2. Client commercial models

### Performance
Client pays per accepted outcome:
- Accepted qualified lead.
- Qualified attended appointment.
- Verified sale or conversion.

Use where outputs are measurable and acceptance criteria are objective.

### Hybrid
Client pays:
- Campaign activation/setup fee.
- Variable fee per accepted outcome.
- Optional conversion bonus.

Recommended default to test with small technology businesses.

### Managed retainer
Client pays a recurring fee for defined capacity, activity, outputs, management, and reporting. Performance incentives may be additional.

### Commission share
Client pays LeadVerify a percentage or fixed commission on attributable revenue. Requires robust attribution, collection, reversal, and payout terms.

## 3. Pricing calculation

For each campaign calculate:

`Client revenue`
minus `agent compensation`
minus `direct campaign costs`
minus `payment costs`
minus `other campaign-specific delivery costs`
equals `campaign contribution`

Contribution margin:

`campaign contribution / client revenue × 100`

This is not net profit. Corporate overhead, tax, and other indirect costs remain to be accounted for.

## 4. Agent compensation models

- Fixed task fee.
- Fee per accepted lead.
- Fee per qualified attended appointment.
- Fixed campaign allocation.
- Commission on attributable collected revenue.
- Hybrid fixed plus variable compensation.
- Team-lead override, only where the role and economics justify it.

Compensation terms must specify:
- Currency.
- Amount/rate.
- What triggers earning.
- What evidence is required.
- Review/acceptance process.
- Payment timing.
- Client non-payment implications, if any and legally permissible.
- Refunds, reversals, and clawbacks.
- Dispute process.
- Tax/invoicing responsibilities.

## 5. Commission attribution

Before launch, define:
- What constitutes a sourced lead.
- How duplicate leads are handled.
- How long attribution lasts.
- How lead generation, qualification, appointment setting, and closing contributions are credited.
- What happens when multiple agents touch an opportunity.
- Which system or record is authoritative.
- What evidence the client must provide.
- How refunds/cancellations affect commission.
- How disputes are resolved.

Avoid vague terms such as “commission on any sale from the campaign” without a clear attribution window and evidence standard.

## 6. Working-capital controls

- Prefer activation fees or milestone funding where possible.
- Consider client-funded campaign budgets for agent incentives.
- Set campaign exposure limits.
- Do not pay out uncollected commission unless LeadVerify explicitly approves and funds the risk.
- Keep a reserve for refunds and reversals where appropriate.
- Monitor debtor days and overdue accounts.
- Pause work when contractually permitted payment thresholds are breached.
- Do not use one client's campaign funds to cover another client's obligations without a lawful and clearly documented basis.

## 7. Illustrative example

A campaign earns R20,000:
- Agent compensation: R8,000.
- Direct campaign costs: R3,000.
- LeadVerify campaign contribution: R9,000.
- Contribution margin: 45%.

Illustrative only. It excludes indirect overhead, tax, and other corporate costs.

## 8. Approval rule

A campaign should not be approved until:
- Scope and outcomes are defined.
- Client and product are sufficiently verified.
- Commercial terms are documented.
- Agent compensation is viable and accepted.
- Direct cost and risk are estimated.
- Payment timing and collection risk are approved.
- Attribution and reversals are addressed.
- Required legal/compliance checks are complete.
