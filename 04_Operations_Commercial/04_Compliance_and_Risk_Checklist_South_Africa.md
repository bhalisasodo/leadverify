# LeadVerify — Compliance & Risk Checklist (South Africa)

**This is a planning checklist, not legal advice. Obtain qualified South African legal, tax, and accounting advice before launch.**

## 1. Privacy and personal information
- Map personal information collected from businesses, agents, prospects, and meeting participants.
- Identify the responsible party/operator roles for each processing activity.
- Document purposes and lawful basis for processing.
- Provide appropriate privacy notices.
- Minimise collection and access.
- Define retention and secure deletion.
- Establish data subject request handling.
- Secure processors and subcontractors contractually.
- Assess cross-border transfers and hosting.
- Maintain incident response and breach notification procedures.
- Appoint/confirm Information Officer responsibilities where required.
- Review POPIA and applicable regulations with counsel.

## 2. Direct marketing
- Review POPIA direct marketing requirements, including electronic communications.
- Review applicable channel-specific laws and platform policies.
- Maintain opt-out and suppression mechanisms.
- Record source, channel, contact date, and relevant consent/lawful-basis evidence.
- Ensure client and LeadVerify responsibilities are contractually allocated.
- Do not scrape or use contact data without assessing legality, terms, and reasonable expectations.
- Do not allow agents to bypass suppression or opt-out rules.

## 3. Consumer and advertising rules
- Review Consumer Protection Act applicability.
- Ensure sales claims are accurate and substantiated.
- Provide approved scripts and prohibited claims.
- Define complaint and escalation handling.
- Review sector-specific advertising and disclosure requirements.

## 4. Financial and regulated products
- Screen campaign products for financial services, insurance, credit, investment, crypto-related financial products, and other regulated activities.
- Determine whether LeadVerify or agents require licences, approvals, supervision, or fit-and-proper status.
- For relevant financial services, obtain specialist advice on FAIS and related requirements.
- Do not allocate regulated sales work to unqualified or unauthorised agents.

## 5. Agent and labour matters
- Review independent contractor versus employee classification based on the actual relationship.
- Review BCEA, LRA, tax, UIF, COIDA and other applicable obligations with advisers.
- Use written agreements with clear scope and compensation.
- Avoid relying solely on contract labels.
- Document training, supervision, control, tools, working arrangements, and performance expectations.

## 6. Commercial contracts
- Master services agreement.
- Campaign statement of work.
- Agent agreement.
- Data processing / operator terms where applicable.
- Confidentiality and IP provisions.
- Lead ownership and use rights.
- Commission attribution and audit rights.
- Payment terms and late-payment consequences.
- Refund, cancellation, reversal, and clawback rules.
- Dispute resolution.
- Liability limitations and appropriate insurance review.

## 7. Security
- Role-based access and least privilege.
- RLS and tenant isolation.
- Private file storage.
- MFA for administrators.
- Secrets management.
- Backups and recovery.
- Audit logs.
- Access revocation after campaign completion.
- Incident response plan.
- Vendor security review.

## 8. Risk register

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Client does not pay | Client verification, payment milestones, exposure limits |
| Agents submit poor leads | Defined criteria, review, training, quality monitoring |
| Commission attribution dispute | Contractual attribution rules and audit trail |
| Data misuse | Access limits, training, monitoring, contractual controls |
| Agent misclassification | Legal review of actual operating relationship |
| Regulated product sold unlawfully | Industry screening and eligibility controls |
| Campaign is economically unviable | Unit economics and approval gate |
| Client product cannot fulfil demand | Product and fulfilment due diligence |
| Agent capacity overstated | Availability and active assignment tracking |
