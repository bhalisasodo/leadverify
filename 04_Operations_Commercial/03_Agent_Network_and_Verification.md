# LeadVerify — Agent Network & Verification Framework

## 1. Network principle

Registration is open. Campaign access is controlled.

Anyone may express interest and apply, but only approved agents can view or accept eligible campaigns. Assignment-level permissions control client data access.

## 2. Agent onboarding stages

1. Registered.
2. Application submitted.
3. Verification pending.
4. Skills assessment pending.
5. Approved or rejected.
6. Available / inactive.
7. Assigned to campaign.
8. Performance reviewed.

Suspension and re-verification must be supported.

## 3. Profile information

- Name and contact details.
- Location and timezone.
- Languages.
- Sales experience.
- Industry experience.
- Specialisations.
- Preferred sales channels.
- Availability and capacity.
- Compensation preferences.
- References or work evidence where appropriate.

Collect only information necessary for network operations.

## 4. Verification

Potential checks:
- Identity verification using a proportionate, lawful method.
- Contact verification.
- Reference checks.
- Experience validation.
- Skills/practical assessment.
- Contract and policy acceptance.

Do not store raw identity documents by default. If document storage is necessary, restrict access, define retention, encrypt appropriately, and obtain legal/privacy review.

## 5. Initial assessment rubric (proposed)

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Communication | 20% |
| Sales competency | 25% |
| Reliability | 20% |
| Industry knowledge | 15% |
| Practical assessment | 20% |

Weights are proposed and should be tested. Record rubric version and evidence.

## 6. Ongoing performance

Track separately from initial assessment:
- Deliverable acceptance rate.
- Timeliness.
- Attendance and reliability.
- Client feedback.
- Compliance with campaign guidelines.
- Conversion outcomes where sample size and attribution are sufficient.
- Repeat assignment eligibility.

Do not publish misleading rankings from small or incomparable samples.

## 7. Agent specialisations

- Lead researcher.
- Sales development representative.
- Lead qualifier.
- Appointment setter.
- Account executive / closer.
- Account manager.

An agent may have multiple approved specialisations.

## 8. Agent opportunity information

Before accepting an assignment, agents should see:
- Client/product description sufficient to assess fit.
- Campaign objective.
- Role and responsibilities.
- Required skills.
- Deliverables and acceptance criteria.
- Time commitment and duration.
- Compensation model and amount/rate.
- Payment trigger and timing.
- Required tools and training.
- Data handling obligations.
- Restrictions and escalation route.

## 9. Contractor and employment considerations

The label “independent contractor” is not determinative. The actual working relationship and applicable law matter. Obtain South African legal advice on classification, control, working hours, exclusivity, equipment, remuneration, tax, and sector-specific requirements before scale.
