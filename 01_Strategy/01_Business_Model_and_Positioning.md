# LeadVerify — Business Model & Positioning

## 1. Executive summary

LeadVerify is a managed, technology-focused sales execution network. It connects growing technology businesses with verified sales professionals and manages the execution of sales campaigns.

LeadVerify owns the client relationship, campaign design, agent selection, delivery oversight, quality assurance, reporting, and commercial administration. Independent sales professionals perform campaign work under defined assignments and agreements.

The long-term ambition is a technology-enabled distributed sales network. The first product release should support managed delivery and use internal/manual matching. Public self-service hiring is out of scope for the initial release.

## 2. Positioning

### Proposed positioning statement

**LeadVerify — Your distributed sales team. Managed for performance.**

LeadVerify gives growing technology businesses access to verified sales professionals who execute lead generation, prospecting, qualification, appointment setting, and sales conversion. LeadVerify manages campaign strategy, agent coordination, execution oversight, quality control, and reporting.

### Value propositions

**For technology businesses**
- Access sales capacity without recruiting and managing a complete internal team.
- Engage one accountable managed-service provider.
- Choose campaign structures that align cost with defined outputs or performance.
- See campaign activity, accepted deliverables, and outcomes.

**For sales professionals**
- Open registration to a curated network.
- Access legitimate, clearly described campaign assignments.
- See responsibilities, qualification criteria, compensation terms, and payment conditions before accepting.
- Build a verifiable work and performance history.

**For LeadVerify**
- Deliver campaigns using flexible capacity.
- Build reusable campaign processes and industry knowledge.
- Develop proprietary operational and performance data.
- Earn through campaign fees, outcome fees, retainers, and agreed commissions.

## 3. Strategic decisions

| Area | Decision |

## 4. Initial market focus

Target small technology businesses that have a viable product or service and need more sales capacity.

### Initial verticals (proposed priority)
1. SaaS and software companies.
2. IT and managed service providers.
3. Cybersecurity service providers.
4. AI and automation companies.
5. Software development studios.
6. Digital infrastructure and hosting providers.

The founders selected technology as the initial industry. The order above is a recommended starting hypothesis, not validated market research.

### Ideal customer profile

A suitable initial client:
- Has a functioning product or established service.
- Has paying customers or evidence of demand.
- Can describe its ideal customer profile.
- Can fulfil additional customer demand.
- Has a sales process that can be documented.
- Can provide product information, sales materials, and timely feedback.
- Can agree to transparent campaign economics and attribution.
- Has the financial capacity to meet its obligations.

### Client disqualification signals
- Unproven or misleading product claims.
- No fulfilment capacity.
- Unclear pricing or target market.
- Unrealistic sales expectations.
- Refusal to define accepted deliverables.
- Unwillingness to provide attribution or outcome data.
- Inability or unwillingness to agree clear payment terms.
- Regulated product/service without appropriate authorisations.

## 5. Service architecture

LeadVerify can sell individual modules or combine them into managed campaigns.

### A. Lead Intelligence
- Prospect identification.
- Company research.
- Contact and decision-maker research.
- Data verification.
- Prospect database preparation.

Primary outputs: verified prospect records and qualified target lists.

### B. Outbound Sales
- Cold calling and other approved outreach.
- Email and other approved channel outreach.
- Follow-up sequences.
- Initial interest assessment.
- Lead qualification.

Primary outputs: documented outreach activity and qualified opportunities.

### C. Appointment Generation
- Meeting scheduling.
- Calendar coordination.
- Meeting confirmations.
- Attendance follow-up.
- Meeting qualification and validation.

Primary outputs: qualified and, where contracted, attended appointments.

### D. Sales Conversion
- Discovery calls.
- Product demonstrations.
- Presentations.
- Objection handling.
- Negotiation and closing.
- Follow-up and handover.

Primary outputs: verified conversions, contracts, and attributable revenue.

The service and campaign scope must be explicit. Do not imply that every agent is qualified or authorised to perform every activity.

## 6. Operating model

1. Business submits a campaign request.
2. LeadVerify reviews business legitimacy, product viability, target market, scope, and economics.
3. LeadVerify clarifies deliverables, KPIs, budget, commission, attribution, and payment terms.
4. A campaign agreement / statement of work is approved.
5. LeadVerify selects and assigns suitable verified agents.
6. Agents receive only the information and access needed for their tasks.
7. Agents execute and submit activities/deliverables.
8. LeadVerify reviews quality and accepts or rejects deliverables against stated criteria.
9. LeadVerify reports campaign results to the client.
10. LeadVerify invoices / reconciles client revenue and calculates agent compensation.
11. Campaign is closed, renewed, or adjusted.

Clients interact with LeadVerify as the accountable service provider. Direct client-to-agent hiring is not part of the MVP.

## 7. Revenue model

### Performance campaigns
Client pays for pre-defined accepted outcomes, such as a qualified lead, qualified attended appointment, or successful sale.

### Hybrid campaigns
Client pays a campaign activation/setup fee plus variable outcome fees and/or a conversion bonus. This is the recommended default hypothesis because it helps cover initial setup and delivery costs while aligning incentives.

### Managed retainers
Client pays a recurring monthly management fee for agreed campaign capacity, activity, outputs, and reporting. Performance bonuses may be added.

### Commission-sharing
Client pays LeadVerify an agreed commission on attributable sales. LeadVerify allocates compensation among agents and retains its agreed share.

### Potential later revenue streams
- Enterprise campaign management.
- Premium verification or assessment services, if commercially justified.
- Business subscriptions for additional reporting or campaign tools.
- Transaction / platform fees if a future marketplace model is introduced.

Subscriptions and agent-paid fees are not required for the MVP and should not be introduced without validation.

## 8. Campaign unit economics

Every campaign must have a campaign-level financial model:
- Expected and contracted client revenue.
- Agent compensation.
- Direct campaign costs.
- Management and quality-assurance costs.
- Payment processing costs.
- Refunds, reversals, and commission clawback exposure.
- Expected collection timing.
- Contribution amount and contribution margin.
- Working-capital requirement and risk owner.

**Campaign acceptance rule:** Do not approve a campaign unless the expected economics cover agent compensation, direct costs, management effort, payment risk, and a reasonable contribution for LeadVerify.

Expected commission is not cash. Do not make unconditional agent payment promises based solely on future or uncollected client revenue.

## 9. Defensibility

Potential long-term advantages:
- Curated, verified specialist sales network.
- Reliable campaign execution processes.
- Campaign and agent performance history.
- Industry-specific playbooks and qualification standards.
- Quality assurance and attribution infrastructure.
- Repeat client relationships and trusted supply.

The number of registered agents alone is not a competitive advantage. Productive, reliable capacity and measurable outcomes are.

## 10. Key assumptions to validate

- Small technology businesses will pay for managed sales execution.
- Hybrid pricing is more acceptable than a large upfront deposit.
- A sufficient pool of agents will accept the proposed compensation.
- LeadVerify can maintain quality across distributed agents.
- Campaigns can be delivered with positive contribution margin.
- Clients will provide outcome data and pay on agreed terms.
- Technology/SaaS is a sufficiently focused initial vertical.

## 11. Success measures

- Paying clients and repeat campaigns.
- Campaign contribution margin.
- Time to staff and launch a campaign.
- Cost per accepted qualified lead.
- Cost per qualified attended appointment.
- Lead acceptance rate.
- Meeting attendance rate.
- Client conversion rate where data is available.
- Agent productivity, reliability, and retention.
- Client payment collection period.
- Client retention and expansion.
- Percentage of campaign operations that can run without founder intervention.
